(2) if you find yourself taking a break to stretch, go for a walk, or do smth that helps recharge --> genuine need for a break

    (1) if you feel more anxious and scattered --> you're using breaks as a way to avoid the assignment
    (2) if you feel more energized and focused --> you needed that break.

physical reactions are your body signaling to you that something is not right. however, your mental state can worsen once those physical reactions start.

consider writing down strategies and positive reminders on a sticky note and stick it somewhere you will see it often. that way, when you start to feel overwhelmed, you'll have a little reminder to take a step back and refocus.
